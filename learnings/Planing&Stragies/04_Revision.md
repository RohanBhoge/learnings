### Is Weekly Revision the Best Way to Use Spaced Repetition?

To answer your direct question: **No, revising *all* topics every week is not Spaced Repetition.** It's a method called "massed practice" or "cramming."

* **Your Suggestion (Weekly "Revise All"):** This involves learning everything first and then having one huge revision session every 7 days. While it's better than not revising at all, it's highly inefficient. Your brain has to work very hard to recall information it hasn't seen in a while, and you'll spend a lot of time on topics you already know well, neglecting the ones you're about to forget.

* **True Spaced Repetition (The method in your table):** The table you provided is the *perfect* example of how to do Spaced Repetition correctly. You revise specific topics at scientifically chosen, increasing intervals (e.g., 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days). This interrupts the "forgetting curve" at the perfect moment, strengthening your memory each time with minimal effort.

**My strong recommendation as your learning coach is to use the True Spaced Repetition method outlined in the table you sent.** It is far more effective for building long-term, durable knowledge.

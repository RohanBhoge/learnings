Here are some of the **best YouTube podcasts and episodes to help you develop social skills, including communication and behavior**:

- **Body Language Expert: Stop Using This, It’s Making People Dislike You**  
  By Vanessa Van Edwards on The Diary Of A CEO  
  Focus: Science-backed social skills and communication tips  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHUrdELKjDw)[1]

- **#1 Communication Expert: If You Get Anxious Around Other PeopleATCH THIS!  
  By Jay Shetty Podcast  
  Focus: Overcoming social anxiety & mastering conversation  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ru44DngJYoA)[2]

- **Communicate with Confidence: Master Every Conversation**  
  By Mel Robbins  
  Focus: Practical strategies to improve communication, boost confidence, and handle tough conversations  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZUCB3M_1Qp4)[3]

- **The Behaviour Expert: Instantly Read Any Room & Hack Your Discipline!**  
  By Chase Hughes on The Diary Of A CEO  
  Focus: Reading rooms, discipline, and behavioral communication  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvjR9GM2kX8)[4]

- **Emotional Intelligence Coach: How To Deal With Emotions & Relationships**  
  With Shivam & Raj Shamani  
  Focus: Emotional intelligence, relationships, anger control  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YcGXViwXItM)[5]

- **Master the Art of Influence + Communication (Become MAGNETIC)**  
  By Charles Duhigg & Lewis Howes  
  Focus: Influence, compelling communication, and personal magnetism  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k1TqbGSQZVE)[6]

- **Robert Greene: How To Seduce Anyone, Build Confidence & Become Powerful**  
  By The Diary Of A CEO  
  Focus: Power, confidence, strategy, body language  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qv70RMUFlu0)[7]

- **Social Skills & Life Lessons – Lingokids Podcasts** (great for beginners/young learners)  
  Focus: Social skills and life lessons formatted as stories  
  [Listen here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTbwBjnA1Yo&list=PLUyB8x_hbKmzj3AxsifEGujif0stOe5En)[8]

- **Improve Your Social Skills in Under 30 Minutes with Ramit Sethi**  
  Practical advice in an easy-to-understand playlist for adults  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1eOKwU0LLTE&list=PLPZCUlZ2z01ldWwZRckoDRElC9GFq4iGF)[9]

- **Social Skills Coaching – What Makes Assertive Communicators Different**  
  Focus: Assertive communication skills in short podcast and coaching formats  
  [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/unjC7dwq3Ms)[10]

These episodes and podcast channels will help you with both practical tips and in-depth discussions for **better communication, social confidence, behavior management, and emotional intelligence**.

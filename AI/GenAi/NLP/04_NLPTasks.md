### **Short Notes on Common NLP Tasks**

* **Text Classification**
    * **Goal:** Assigning a predefined label or category to a piece of text.
    * **Example:** Classifying an email as `Spam` or `Not Spam`, or determining if a movie review is `Positive` or `Negative`.

* **Machine Translation**
    * **Goal:** Automatically converting text from a source language to a target language.
    * **Example:** Using Google Translate to translate a sentence from English to French.

* **Text Similarity**
    * **Goal:** Measuring how semantically similar two pieces of text are.
    * **Example:** Identifying duplicate questions on a forum or finding related news articles.

* **Language Modeling**
    * **Goal:** Predicting the next word in a sequence based on the preceding text. This is a core task that powers many other applications.
    * **Example:** The autocomplete feature on your smartphone keyboard suggesting the next word as you type.

* **Information Extraction**
    * **Goal:** Pulling structured information (like names, dates, locations) from unstructured text.
    * **Example:** Automatically extracting the names of people and companies mentioned in a news article.

* **Text Summarization**
    * **Goal:** Generating a concise and coherent summary of a longer document.
    * **Example:** Creating a one-paragraph abstract for a long research paper.

* **Information Retrieval**
    * **Goal:** Finding relevant documents or information from a large dataset in response to a user's query.
    * **Example:** A search engine like Google returning a list of relevant websites for your search query.

* **Topic Modeling**
    * **Goal:** Discovering abstract topics that are present in a collection of documents.
    * **Example:** Analyzing thousands of customer reviews to find recurring themes like "shipping issues," "product quality," and "customer support."

* **Chat Bots**
    * **Goal:** Creating an AI agent that can converse with a human user via text.
    * **Example:** A customer service bot on a website that answers frequently asked questions.

* **Voice Assistants**
    * **Goal:** Advanced systems that understand and respond to human speech to perform tasks. They combine speech recognition with multiple NLP tasks.
    * **Example:** Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple's Siri.
